# RepRush — Product Requirements Document & API Contract
**Version 1.0 | May 2026 | Confidential**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

RepRush is an Android application that serves as a complete gym operating system. It manages both the business side of running a gym (member registration, membership billing, attendance tracking, revenue reporting) and the fitness side of being a gym member (AI-generated workout plans, session logging, gamification, progress analytics, workout volume heatmap). The app operates as a single-gym platform where all users are either members of RepRush gym or administrators managing it.

The app has two distinct user roles accessed from the same login screen. Admins see a management dashboard. Members see a fitness experience with AI-generated workout plans from Google Gemini, a full exercise library with images from the Wger API, real-time workout logging, a GitHub-style workout volume heatmap, points and leaderboard competition, and comprehensive progress charts.

The core differentiator is the AI Trainer model. RepRush positions itself as an AI-trainer-based gym where no physical trainer is needed. Members receive personalized workout programs generated by Gemini AI using real exercises from the Wger database. An optional AI chatbot allows members to ask training questions in natural language.

---

## 2. Product Vision

RepRush aims to be a complete replacement for the combination of tools a small gym currently uses: a spreadsheet for member tracking, a notebook for attendance, a calculator for billing, and a trainer for programming. All of these collapse into one app that both the gym owner and every member carry in their pocket.

For the gym owner, RepRush eliminates administrative overhead. For the member, it eliminates the need for a personal trainer. For the gym as a business, the gamification layer drives member retention which directly translates to sustained revenue.

## 3. Target Users and Personas

### 3.1 Admin Persona — The Gym Owner

Name: Karim, 35, owns a small neighborhood gym with 40–80 members. Currently tracks membership in Excel, collects cash payments, and writes receipts by hand. Loses track of who has paid, who is expiring, and who has stopped coming.

**Needs:** A digital system that handles registration, billing, and attendance without hiring office staff.

### 3.2 Member Persona — The Regular Gym-Goer

Name: Aryan, 22, university student. Trains 4–5 days per week but has no structured program. Cannot afford a personal trainer. Wants a real workout plan, a way to track progress visually, and something that makes training feel competitive.

**Needs:** AI-generated programs, clear visual progress tracking (including a heatmap of workout history), and social competition with other gym members.

---

## 4. Problem Statement

**The business problem:** Small gym owners manage everything manually. Member records are in spreadsheets. Payment tracking is unreliable. There is no easy way to know which members are about to expire, which have stopped coming, or how much revenue was collected this month.

**The member problem:** Most gym members have no structured workout program. Personal trainers are expensive. Free fitness apps provide generic plans with no images, no visual workout history, and no social competition with people they actually know. Members lose motivation within months.

RepRush solves both problems in one application. The admin gets a full business management tool. The member gets an AI-powered training experience with gamification and visual history.

## 5. Functional Requirements

### 5.1 Authentication and Role Routing

- **FR-01:** The app shall support Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication for both roles.
- **FR-02:** After login, the app shall read the user's role field from Firestore and route to either the admin dashboard or the member home screen.
- **FR-03:** New users who sign in for the first time shall be created as members with a pending membership status and routed to the waiting screen.
- **FR-04:** Admin accounts shall be pre-created in Firestore manually. There is no in-app admin registration flow.
- **FR-05:** Users with pending status shall see a waiting screen with gym contact information until approved by an admin.
- **FR-06:** Suspended or expired members shall see a status screen explaining their situation with instructions to contact the gym.

### 5.2 Admin — Member Registration and Management

- **FR-07:** Admin shall view a list of all pending registration requests with applicant name, email, and submission date.
- **FR-08:** Admin shall approve or reject pending registrations. Approval requires selecting a membership package.
- **FR-09:** Admin shall manually register a new member on their behalf for walk-in registrations by entering their details.
- **FR-10:** Admin shall view a complete member directory with search by name, filter by status (active, expired, suspended, pending), and sort by name or join date.
- **FR-11:** Admin shall view any member's full profile including personal details, current membership package, payment history, attendance log, and workout summary stats.
- **FR-12:** Admin shall suspend, reactivate, or permanently remove any member account.

### 5.3 Admin — Membership Package Management

- **FR-13:** Admin shall create membership packages with a name, price, duration in days, and description.
- **FR-14:** Admin shall edit existing package details. Changes shall not affect members currently on that package.
- **FR-15:** Admin shall deactivate packages so they no longer appear as options for new member assignments.
- **FR-16:** Admin shall assign any active package to a member during registration or renewal.

### 5.4 Admin — Payment Recording and Receipts

- **FR-17:** Admin shall record a payment against a member's account with amount, payment method (cash, bank transfer, mobile banking), and date.
- **FR-18:** On payment recording, the system shall generate a digital receipt with gym name, member name, amount, payment date, package name, coverage period start and end, and a unique receipt number.
- **FR-19:** The receipt shall be delivered as an in-app notification to the member's notification inbox immediately after recording.
- **FR-20:** The receipt shall also be sent to the member's registered email address automatically.
- **FR-21:** Admin shall view a complete payment log filterable by date range, member, and payment method.
- **FR-22:** Admin shall void an incorrectly recorded payment with a reason note.

### 5.5 Admin — Membership Expiry and Renewal

- **FR-23:** The admin dashboard shall prominently display memberships expiring within 7 days and memberships already expired.
- **FR-24:** Admin shall configure a grace period in days during which an expired member still has app access before automatic suspension.
- **FR-25:** The system shall automatically suspend members whose membership has been expired beyond the configured grace period.
- **FR-26:** The system shall send automatic in-app notifications to members at 7 days, 3 days, and 0 days before membership expiry.
- **FR-27:** Admin shall send a manual reminder notification to any specific member from their profile screen.
- **FR-28:** On renewal, admin records a new payment and assigns the package for the new period. The member's full history is preserved and not overwritten.

### 5.6 Admin — Attendance Tracking

- **FR-29:** Admin shall mark any member as present for the current day from a quick-access attendance screen.
- **FR-30:** The admin dashboard shall show a real-time count and list of members checked in today.
- **FR-31:** Admin shall view any individual member's full attendance history with visit counts per week and per month.
- **FR-32:** Admin shall view aggregate attendance statistics: average visits per member per week, busiest days of the week, and a list of members who have not visited in over 14 days.

### 5.7 Admin — Announcements

- **FR-33:** Admin shall post gym-wide announcements with a title and body text.
- **FR-34:** Posted announcements shall appear as in-app notifications for all active members and as a dismissible card on the member home screen.
- **FR-35:** Admin shall delete old announcements from the active list.

### 5.8 Admin — Dashboard and Revenue

- **FR-36:** The admin home screen shall display six live metrics: total active members, pending registrations count, memberships expiring within 7 days, members checked in today, revenue collected this month, and revenue collected this year.
- **FR-37:** Each metric card shall be tappable and navigate to the relevant detailed screen.

### 5.9 Member — Exercise Library

- **FR-38:** On first launch after member approval, the app shall sync the full exercise catalog from the Wger API and cache it in Room. A DataStore flag shall prevent repeat syncs.
- **FR-39:** Each exercise shall store name, primary muscle, secondary muscles, equipment, category, exercise image URL, thumbnail URL, and muscle diagram URL.
- **FR-40:** The exercise library shall be searchable by name and filterable by muscle group and equipment type.
- **FR-41:** Members shall be able to add custom exercises with a name, muscle group, equipment, and category.

### 5.10 Member — AI Workout Plan Generation

- **FR-42:** The app shall present an 8-step questionnaire collecting goal, days per week, split type, session duration, equipment access, fitness level, plan duration in weeks, and injury restrictions.
- **FR-43:** The app shall call the Gemini 1.5 Flash API with all questionnaire answers and the filtered list of exercise names from Room that match the user's available equipment.
- **FR-44:** The prompt shall explicitly instruct Gemini to return only valid JSON and to use only exercise names from the provided list.
- **FR-45:** The app shall validate and parse the JSON response against schema version 1, create the plan records in Room across workout_plans, plan_days, and plan_exercises tables, and present a summary screen for member confirmation.
- **FR-46:** Members shall be able to save multiple plans and designate one as the active plan at any time.

### 5.11 Member — AI Trainer Chatbot (Bonus Feature)

- **FR-47:** Members shall access a persistent chat interface to ask the AI trainer questions about their program, exercise substitutions, or general fitness advice.
- **FR-48:** Each Gemini API call for the chatbot shall include a system prompt with the member's current active plan, fitness level, and goals as context.
- **FR-49:** All chat message history shall be persisted locally in Room so members can scroll back through past conversations.

### 5.12 Member — Workout Logging

- **FR-50:** Members shall start a session pre-loaded from the day's scheduled plan or as a blank session.
- **FR-51:** Each session shall support adding and removing exercises, adding and removing sets, logging weight and reps per set, and marking sets as warmup (excluded from points, volume, and PR calculations).
- **FR-52:** A rest timer shall start automatically after each set completion and run in a foreground service notification with +30s and Skip actions so it operates with the screen off.
- **FR-53:** Sessions under 10 minutes shall not earn attendance points but shall still be saved normally.
- **FR-54:** The app shall auto-save session state to Room every 30 seconds as a crash recovery mechanism.

### 5.13 Member — PR Detection and Records

- **FR-55:** On session completion, the app shall compare every working set against historical maximum weight for that exercise at that rep count.
- **FR-56:** New PRs shall be recorded automatically in the pr_records table with Epley-estimated 1RM at time of achievement.
- **FR-57:** PR history per exercise shall be viewable on the exercise detail page as a chronological list and a line chart.

### 5.14 Member — Gamification

- **FR-58:** Points shall be awarded per session: 50 pts attendance (sessions 10+ minutes only), 10 pts per exercise completed, 2 pts per working set completed, 100 pts per new PR. Daily cap of 500 pts before streak bonuses.
- **FR-59:** Streak bonuses shall be awarded on completion of a session: 200 pts at 7-day streak, 1000 pts at 30-day streak.
- **FR-60:** Streak shall be based on planned workout days only. Missing a scheduled day resets the streak to zero.
- **FR-61:** The app shall support at least 11 achievement badges with documented unlock conditions.
- **FR-62:** A gym-wide monthly leaderboard shall rank all opted-in members by points earned in the current calendar month.
- **FR-63:** Members shall be able to opt out of the leaderboard from Settings. Opted-out members are excluded from the ranking but retain all other features.

### 5.15 Member — Workout Volume Heatmap

- **FR-64:** The member home screen and progress tab shall display a GitHub-style workout volume heatmap using the Kizitonwose HeatMapCalendar library.
- **FR-65:** Each day cell's color intensity shall be determined by that day's total lifting volume (sum of weight multiplied by reps for all working sets), normalized against the user's personal maximum volume day in the displayed range.
- **FR-66:** Days with no session shall display the surface variant color. Days with a session shall display a color ranging from primary container (low volume) to primary (high volume) based on intensity.
- **FR-67:** The heatmap shall display the past 6 months by default. Month labels shall appear above each column group and day-of-week labels (M, W, F) on the left side.
- **FR-68:** Tapping a day cell shall show a small tooltip displaying the date, total volume lifted, and number of exercises completed on that day.

### 5.16 Member — Progress and Analytics

- **FR-69:** The Progress tab shall contain three sub-tabs: Overview, Body, and Strength.
- **FR-70:** Overview tab shall show the workout volume heatmap, a workout history list, and a weekly volume per muscle group bar chart.
- **FR-71:** Body tab shall show daily body weight log entry, a line chart with 7-day rolling average, and stat chips (starting, current, monthly change, all-time change, lowest).
- **FR-72:** Strength tab shall show the Strength Score (sum of estimated 1RM across 5 key compound lifts) with a history line chart, 5 per-lift trend cards each with current 1RM and 30-day change, and a 12-week total volume bar chart.
- **FR-73:** Members shall be able to view a history calendar showing workout attendance as color-coded days (completed, missed, rest day).
- **FR-74:** Members shall be able to tap any past session to view the full session detail including all exercises, sets, warmup flags, and PR flags.

### 5.17 Member — Membership Visibility

- **FR-75:** The member home screen shall display a membership status card showing current package name, expiry date, and a countdown of days remaining.
- **FR-76:** The membership card color shall change based on remaining days: neutral for 10+ days, amber for 3–10 days, red for under 3 days or expired.
- **FR-77:** Members shall view their complete payment history with all receipts accessible from their profile screen.

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements

### 6.1 Performance

- **NFR-01:** App cold start to home screen under 2 seconds on a mid-range device (Snapdragon 680 class).
- **NFR-02:** Gemini API responses shall be awaited asynchronously. A loading state is displayed while waiting. No ANR risk.
- **NFR-03:** Room database queries shall complete within 300ms.
- **NFR-04:** All list screens shall maintain 60fps scrolling on mid-range devices.
- **NFR-05:** The HeatMapCalendar shall render within one frame after data is available. Volume data is pre-computed in the ViewModel before binding.

### 6.2 Security

- **NFR-06:** All Firestore data shall be protected by security rules. Members cannot read other members' personal or payment data.
- **NFR-07:** Admin-only Firestore write operations shall be enforced by security rules that verify the requesting user's role field.
- **NFR-08:** The Gemini API key shall be stored in local.properties and accessed via BuildConfig. It shall never be committed to version control.
- **NFR-09:** Google Sign-In tokens shall be managed entirely by the Firebase Auth SDK and never stored or handled manually.

### 6.3 Offline Support

- **NFR-10:** All member fitness features (session logging, plan viewing, exercise library, history, heatmap) shall function fully without internet. All data is read from Room.
- **NFR-11:** Firestore offline persistence shall be enabled. Writes queue locally and sync automatically on reconnection.
- **NFR-12:** Admin features that read Firestore shall show cached data with a sync status indicator when offline.
- **NFR-13:** The app shall never display a network error on screens that can be served from Room cache.

### 6.4 Accessibility

- **NFR-14:** All interactive elements shall have contentDescription attributes for TalkBack compatibility.
- **NFR-15:** All text shall meet WCAG AA contrast ratio (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text).
- **NFR-16:** All tap targets shall be a minimum of 48x48dp.
- **NFR-17:** The app shall support Android system font scaling without layout breakage.
- **NFR-18:** The heatmap day cells shall have contentDescription including the date and volume so TalkBack users can navigate them.

### 6.5 Compatibility

- **NFR-19:** Minimum SDK: Android 8.0 (API 26). Target SDK: latest stable.
- **NFR-20:** The app shall support both light and dark mode via Material 3 DayNight theme.

## 8. External API Contracts

### 8.1 Gemini 1.5 Flash API

**Used for:** AI workout plan generation and optional trainer chatbot.

**Endpoint:** `https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/models/gemini-1.5-flash:generateContent`

**Authentication:** API key as query parameter. Stored in `local.properties`, accessed via `BuildConfig.GEMINI_API_KEY`.

**Free tier limits:** 15 requests per minute, 1 million tokens per day. Sufficient for development and demo.

#### Plan Generation Prompt Structure

The app constructs the prompt as follows:

```
You are a professional fitness trainer. Generate a workout plan using ONLY the 
exercises from the list below. Return ONLY valid JSON with no explanation, 
markdown, or text outside the JSON object.

Member profile:
- Goal: {goal}
- Days per week: {daysPerWeek}
- Split type: {splitType}
- Session duration: {sessionDuration} minutes
- Equipment available: {equipment}
- Fitness level: {fitnessLevel}
- Plan duration: {weeks} weeks
- Injuries or restrictions: {injuries}

Available exercises (use ONLY these names exactly as written):
{exerciseNamesList}

Return this exact JSON structure:
{
  "schema_version": 1,
  "plan_name": "string",
  "goal": "string",
  "weeks": number,
  "days_per_week": number,
  "schedule": [
    {
      "day_number": number,
      "day_label": "string",
      "exercises": [
        {
          "exercise_name": "string",
          "sets": number,
          "reps": "string",
          "rest_seconds": number,
          "notes": "string or null"
        }
      ]
    }
  ]
}
```

#### Validation Rules on Import

| Field | Required | Type | Validation Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| schema_version | Yes | number | Must equal 1 |
| plan_name | Yes | string | Non-empty, max 100 chars |
| goal | Yes | string | Non-empty |
| weeks | Yes | number | Integer 1–52 |
| days_per_week | Yes | number | Integer 1–7 |
| schedule | Yes | array | Non-empty |
| day_number | Yes | number | Integer >= 1 |
| day_label | Yes | string | Non-empty |
| exercises | Yes | array | Non-empty per day |
| exercise_name | Yes | string | Must match an exercise name in Room exactly |
| sets | Yes | number | Integer 1–20 |
| reps | Yes | string | Non-empty, e.g. 8-10 or 5 |
| rest_seconds | Yes | number | Integer 15–600 |
| notes | No | string or null | Max 500 chars if present |

### 8.2 Wger REST API

**Used for:** Populating the full exercise library with professional exercise data, images, and muscle diagrams on first launch.

**Base URL:** `https://wger.de/api/v2/`

**Authentication:** None required (public API).

| Endpoint | Method | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `/exercise/?format=json&language=2&limit=100&offset=N` | GET | Fetch all English exercises, paginated |
| `/exerciseimage/?format=json&limit=100&offset=N` | GET | Fetch all exercise images, paginated |
| `/exercisecategory/?format=json` | GET | Fetch muscle group categories |
| `/muscle/?format=json` | GET | Fetch muscle definitions for diagram mapping |

**Sync strategy:**
- Called once on first app launch after member approval.
- All endpoints paginated using limit and offset until the results array is empty.
- Exercises joined to images by exercise ID (wgerId).
- All data saved to Room. Wger is never called again during normal use.
- DataStore flag `isLibrarySynced` prevents repeat syncs.
- Exercise names normalized (trimmed, title-cased) before saving to ensure exact Gemini prompt matching.

### 8.3 Kizitonwose Calendar — HeatMapCalendar

**Used for:** Workout volume heatmap on the member home screen and progress overview tab.

**Dependency:** `implementation "com.kizitonwose.calendar:view:2.5.0"`

**Integration pattern:**
- Room query returns a `Map<LocalDate, Float>` of date to total working volume for the past 26 weeks.
- ViewModel normalizes each value to an intensity float (0.0–1.0) based on the user's personal maximum volume day in the range.
- The Fragment binds the intensity map to the HeatMapCalendar using the day binder callback.
- Color is interpolated between Surface Variant (empty), Primary Container (low volume), and Primary (high volume) using `ArgbEvaluator`.
- Tapping a cell shows a tooltip with date and volume. Implemented via `monthScrollListener` and touch event handling provided by the library.

### 8.4 Email Receipts

When admin records a payment, the system sends the receipt to the member's email. Implementation: JavaMail API called from a Kotlin coroutine on `Dispatchers.IO`. Email delivery is best-effort. In-app notification receipt delivery is the guaranteed primary channel. Email failure is silent and does not block the payment recording operation.
